Your visit, answered

We want every guest to know what to expect and feel comfortable asking for what they need.

What makes Miraya Wellness different from other spa services?

Our trained male therapists focus on attentive, professional care, client comfort, hygiene, and respect for privacy. We aim to make each visit a calm, welcoming experience, with pressure and focus areas discussed around your preferences.

Are the male therapists professional and safe?

Yes. Our therapists are trained professionals who follow standards for hygiene, professionalism, and client confidentiality. Your comfort and privacy matter throughout the session. Please share any preferences or concerns before treatment, and let your therapist know if you would like the pressure adjusted or a pause.

Why might I choose a male therapist?

Some guests prefer firmer pressure for muscle relaxation, while others prefer a gentler touch. The right pressure depends on your comfort and treatment goals, not on assumptions; discuss your preferences with your therapist so the session can be adjusted to you.

Which treatments can I book?

Our listed menu includes Jacuzzi, Four Hand, Thai, Deep Tissue, Hot Oil, Swedish, and Full Body Massage. You can review current durations and prices on our services and pricing menu. Other experiences and packages should be confirmed with us before booking.

Are couples welcome?

Yes, couples are welcome. Contact us before your visit so we can discuss your preferred treatment and arrange the booking.

How do I request an appointment?

Use the booking form on our contact page or call +91 9981979259. Share your preferred service, date, and time; we will contact you to confirm the request.

Where are you located, and when are you open?

We are at 71 Princetone Estate Rd, DLF Phase 5, Sector 43, Gurugram. The website lists our hours as 24 hours a day, seven days a week. Please call ahead to confirm availability for your preferred time.

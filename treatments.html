Explore treatment styles

These descriptions introduce approaches featured in our wellness brochure. Availability and pricing for any option not shown on the current menu should be confirmed with our team.

Relaxation massage

A gentle approach using long, gliding strokes and light-to-medium pressure. It is intended to ease everyday tension and create a calmer, more restful experience.

Tell your therapist which areas you would like to focus on and what pressure feels comfortable.

Aromatherapy

A wellness approach that pairs massage with the aroma of essential oils. The brochure describes oils as part of the experience; ask us which options are currently available.

Please mention scent sensitivities or skin concerns before your session.

Swedish massage

A classic full-body massage using flowing strokes and a gentler rhythm. It can be a comfortable starting point if you prefer a soothing, general massage.

Swedish Massage is listed on our current menu with 60- and 90-minute options.

Sports massage

A more targeted soft-tissue approach for active routines and areas that feel tight after repetitive movement or training. Pressure and focus should be discussed before treatment.

Contact us to confirm current availability and pricing before requesting this option.

Choosing your session: Let us know your preferred pressure, focus areas, and any sensitivities when you book. Massage is a wellness service and is not a substitute for medical advice or treatment.

Spa packages

The brochure introduces the idea of combining treatments in one visit. Package contents, availability, and prices can change, so contact us for current details before booking.

Ask us what combinations are currently available and we will confirm the session details with you.
